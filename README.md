### 📖 Overview
**ASTRONEXA Mission Academy** is an interactive, 100% offline-capable Martian Outpost Trainer designed for young learners and classrooms worldwide. Using genuine NASA mission telemetry and engineering constraints, cadets learn how astronauts survive extreme radiation, freezing planetary nights, and sudden space weather storms on Mars.

---

### 🛠️ Engineering Modules
1. **Module 1 – Outpost Architecture Setup:**  
   Cadets evaluate Earth vs. Space technologies and deploy verified systems: **Sintered Regolith Shielding**, **Closed-Loop Hydroponic Bays**, and **NASA's Fission Surface Power (FSP) Reactor**.
2. **Module 2 – 100 kW Surface Reactor Balancing:**  
   Driven by live **NASA InSight Sol 500** telemetry at Mars Elysium Planitia, cadets balance a strict **100 kW** power budget across oxygen life support, radiation defense, and crop biomass.
3. **Module 3 – Solar Storm Emergency Command:**  
   Modeled on a real **NASA DONKI** Solar Energetic Particle (SEP) storm, auxiliary solar arrays fail and power drops to a **50 kW emergency battery**. Cadets act as flight directors—dimming crop lights to keep radiation shields and baseline oxygen active.

### ✨ Key Features
* **Bilingual Accessibility:** Instant one-click switching between **English** and **Bengali (বাংলা)**.
* **Printable A4 Flight Diploma:** Ink-saving, print-ready commander certificate upon mission completion.
* **100% Offline & Lightweight:** Built with pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with zero external libraries—ready for low-bandwidth classrooms anywhere.

### 🛰️ NASA Open Data & Citations
* **NASA InSight Lander TWINS Archive:** Sol 500 surface temperature (-63°C), atmospheric pressure (~720 Pa), and solar insolation (590 W/m²) at Elysium Planitia.
* **NASA DONKI Database:** Solar Energetic Particle event baseline (`#2024-05-11T02:00:00-SEP-001`).
* **NASA Fission Surface Power (FSP) Project:** 40–100 kW continuous fission power architecture.
* **NASA Human Research Program (HRP):** Closed-loop life support and regolith radiation shielding baselines.
